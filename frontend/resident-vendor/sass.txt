1.Requesting a ticket through the company’s ITSM system can bring a more effective result than trying to fix the blue screen herself. Since Leslie did not change any settings, the problem may be caused by a hardware, software, or system issue that requires proper technical knowledge. Through an ITSM ticket, the IT team can record the problem, identify its cause, provide the correct solution, and prevent the same issue from happening again.

2.An ITSM system can help Rachel organize and systemize all requests from her employees instead of relying on people approaching her personally. Each request can be submitted as a ticket, categorized according to its type and urgency, and assigned to the appropriate person for action. This allows Rachel and her startup to track requests, prioritize important issues, monitor their status, and keep a record of how problems were resolved.

3.I would be more careful about buying important software from a business that does not have an ITSM system. An ITSM system provides a more organized way of handling technical problems, support requests, and customer concerns, which can result in faster and more reliable assistance. Since the software is important for my academic requirements, I would prefer a vendor with a proper support and ticketing system because it gives me greater confidence that installation or technical problems will be addressed efficiently.
