📡 Route Telemetry vs GPS:

The Katok Kalinis fleet management system tracks vehicles using scheduled checkpoint telemetry logs rather than direct device GPS feeds. This protects battery and offline stability while delivering accurate checkpoint arrival and tonnage verification.

🔄 Status Workflow:
  • Available: Unit ready in motorpool or depot standby.
  • On Route: Actively servicing designated barangays or markets.
  • Maintenance: Under repair; requires diagnostic log reason.
  • Out of Service: Decommissioned; requires authorized supervisor reason.
